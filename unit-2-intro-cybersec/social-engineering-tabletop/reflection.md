1. Phishing (Scam email)

- Recognition: The message urgently requests that you log in from a link,
The sender's address looks strange, The message contains Spelling errors.

- Immediate Action: Do not open the link or Do not provide your login credentials.

- Verification: Call the sender or verify the matter from another communication channel.

- Escalation: Notify IT support or supervisor that someone is trying to scam you.

- Recovery: Change your password immediately and Notify IT support or supervisor.

- Prevention: use a multi-factor authentication,
  and check that the employees know what is a scam and inform them about security.

4. Tailgating

Recognition: An unknown person follows you or tries to entry your workplace,
making up excuses like having forgotten their Id or KeyCard

Immediate Action: Do not let a unknown person in without verification and call security guard or cops

Verification: Verify the person's identity and conrifm the person's authorization to go in by contacting you're boss, supervisor or helpdesk

Escalation: Report any suspicious person to your supervisor, notify the company's security/IT support.

Recovery: If an unauthorized person has managed to get in report it and make a security notice to you're company and supervisor.

Prevention: If the company is using access/keycards verify that everyone is using their own
and make sure that the employee's are keeping their access/keycards safe.
If the company gets visitors make sure that every visitor are issued a visitor pass and are supervised the whole time.
Make sure that every employee gets information security and safety training.
Company's offices are kept secure and no-one can get in without access/keycard.
